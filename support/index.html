NesRoomPrivacy Policy

Support

For help with NesRoom, email memtimen@gmail.com.

Include your device model, operating system version, NesRoom version, and steps to reproduce the issue. Please avoid including private information or ROM files.

iCloud sync

Use the same Apple Account on your devices, enable iCloud for NesRoom, and allow time for your library and saves to sync while connected to the internet. On Apple TV, import your games on another supported device first.

支持与反馈

如需帮助，请发邮件至 memtimen@gmail.com，并说明设备型号、系统版本、NesRoom 版本及问题复现步骤。请避免发送个人隐私信息或 ROM 文件。

iCloud 同步

在各设备上使用同一个 Apple 账户，为 NesRoom 启用 iCloud，并在联网时等待游戏库和存档完成同步。Apple TV 的游戏需要先在其他支持的设备上导入。
