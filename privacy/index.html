NesRoom Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Scope

This policy applies to NesRoom on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV, and Apple Vision Pro.

Data used by the app

NesRoom stores the ROM files you import, game titles and publisher information, cover images, save states, save previews, battery saves, and related timestamps to provide your game library and resume gameplay. Display preferences and controller or keyboard settings are stored locally. NesRoom does not require a separate developer account.

Local storage and iCloud

Your game library and saves are stored on your device. When iCloud is available and enabled for NesRoom, the app uses Apple's CloudKit service to sync ROMs, game metadata, cover images, save states, save previews, and battery saves through your Apple Account's private iCloud database across your devices. This content is not uploaded to a server operated by the NesRoom developer. The developer does not receive your Apple Account credentials or access to your private iCloud library through the app. Apple's handling of iCloud data is governed by Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

Files and photos

On supported platforms, NesRoom accesses files you select for import and images you select as game covers. Selected cover images become part of your game library and may sync through iCloud. The app does not scan or upload your entire photo library.

Nearby devices and controllers

The wireless phone-controller feature uses Apple's Multipeer Connectivity framework to discover and connect to nearby devices. A device display name is shared with nearby peers for discovery, and controller button events are sent to connected peers to control gameplay. This feature may connect automatically to nearby NesRoom peers while discovery is active. These events are not sent to a developer-operated server or used for advertising. You can decline or revoke local-network permission where available in system settings, or stop using the wireless controller feature. Physical controller and keyboard input is processed to control the emulator.

Advertising, tracking, and analytics

NesRoom does not include advertising, advertising tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs. The app does not sell your personal data or share it with advertisers. Apple may separately process App Store or system diagnostic information according to your device settings and Apple's privacy policy.

External links

If you choose to open a third-party website linked from the app, that website may process data under its own privacy policy. NesRoom does not control those websites.

Retention and deletion

Your library remains on your device and, when syncing is enabled, in your private iCloud storage until you remove it. You can delete games and their associated saves in NesRoom, and delete individual save states where available. Deletions sync to your other devices when iCloud sync completes. You can manage NesRoom's iCloud access and stored data using the controls available in your device's Apple Account and iCloud settings. Disabling sync or uninstalling the app does not necessarily delete existing iCloud data or device backups; Apple manages backup retention separately.

Support

If you email the developer, your email address and the information you voluntarily provide are used to respond to your request. Please do not send ROM files or other sensitive content unless needed for your request. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to resolve and follow up on your request, or meet legal obligations. You can request deletion of support correspondence by email.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The latest policy will show its updated date. For privacy questions or requests, contact memtimen@gmail.com.

NesRoom 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 10 月 5 日

适用范围

本政策适用于 iPhone、iPad、Mac、Apple TV 和 Apple Vision Pro 上的 NesRoom。

App 使用的数据

NesRoom 保存您导入的 ROM 文件、游戏名称及发行商信息、封面图片、即时存档、存档预览图、电池存档及相关时间信息，用于提供游戏库和恢复游戏进度。显示偏好、手柄及键盘设置保存在本机。NesRoom 无需注册独立的开发者账号。

本地存储与 iCloud

游戏库和存档保存在您的设备上。当 iCloud 可用且已为 NesRoom 启用时，App 通过 Apple 的 CloudKit 服务，将 ROM、游戏信息、封面图片、即时存档、存档预览图和电池存档同步到您的 Apple 账户的私有 iCloud 数据库，并在您的设备之间同步。这些内容不会上传至 NesRoom 开发者运营的服务器。开发者不会通过 App 获取您的 Apple 账户登录凭据或访问您的私有 iCloud 游戏库。Apple 对 iCloud 数据的处理遵循其隐私政策：https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/。

文件与照片

在支持的平台上，NesRoom 访问您主动选择导入的文件，以及您主动选择作为游戏封面的图片。选中的封面图片会成为游戏库的一部分，并可能通过 iCloud 同步。App 不会扫描或上传您的整个照片图库。

附近设备与控制器

手机无线手柄功能使用 Apple 的 Multipeer Connectivity 框架发现并连接附近设备。设备显示名称会分享给附近设备用于发现；手柄按键事件会发送至已连接设备，用于控制游戏。发现功能运行时，可能自动连接附近运行 NesRoom 的设备。按键事件不会发送至开发者运营的服务器，也不用于广告。您可以在系统提供相关设置时拒绝或撤销本地网络权限，或停止使用无线手柄功能。实体手柄和键盘输入用于控制模拟器。

广告、跟踪与统计

NesRoom 不包含广告、广告跟踪或第三方统计 SDK，不出售您的个人数据，也不将其分享给广告商。Apple 可能根据您的设备设置及 Apple 隐私政策，另行处理 App Store 或系统诊断信息。

外部链接

如果您主动打开 App 中链接的第三方网站，该网站可能根据其自身隐私政策处理数据。NesRoom 不控制这些网站。

保存与删除

游戏库保存在您的设备上；启用同步时，也保存在您的私有 iCloud 存储中，直到您将其删除。您可以在 NesRoom 中删除游戏及其关联存档，并在提供相应功能的位置删除单个即时存档。iCloud 同步完成后，删除操作会同步至您的其他设备。您可以通过设备的 Apple 账户及 iCloud 设置中提供的选项管理 NesRoom 的 iCloud 访问和已存储数据。关闭同步或卸载 App 不一定删除已有的 iCloud 数据或设备备份；备份保留由 Apple 另行管理。

支持服务

如果您通过邮件联系开发者，您的邮箱地址和主动提供的信息将用于回复请求。除非解决请求需要，请勿发送 ROM 文件或其他敏感内容。支持邮件仅在解决及跟进请求或履行法律义务所需的期限内保留。您可以通过邮件请求删除支持通信记录。

政策更新与联系方式

App 功能或数据处理方式变化时，本政策可能更新。最新政策会标注更新日期。如有隐私问题或请求，请联系 memtimen@gmail.com。
